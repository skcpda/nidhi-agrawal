Computer vision and cyber-physical systems for long-span bridges.

I am a PhD research scholar with Prof. Shashank Gupta at BITS Pilani. I work on vehicle-bridge interaction modelling, structural health assessment, and digital twins that can see damage before it becomes a failure.

Email CV Google Scholar LinkedIn ORCID

01 About

My research sits where civil infrastructure meets computer vision. I build models that read how vehicles and bridges talk to each other, then use that signal for damage detection on long-span structures.

Before the PhD I completed a B.Tech with Honours in Computer Science at AKTU Lucknow (CGPA 8.65), interned in AI and ML at NIELIT Gorakhpur, and spent a term as a Junior Research Fellow on a BITS CDRF digital-twin project.

Outside the lab I write, spend time outdoors, and play volleyball.

02 Research

Interests: vehicle-bridge interaction modelling, computer vision in vehicles, machine learning, and cyber-physical systems.
