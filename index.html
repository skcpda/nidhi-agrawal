I am a PhD research scholar under the supervision of Prof. Shashank Gupta at the Disruptive Technologies Lab, Department of Computer Science and Information Systems, Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani. My research focuses on computer vision, cyber-physical systems, and vehicle-bridge interaction modelling for long-span bridge assessment.

I work on digital twins and transfer learning for structural damage detection, including a BITS Cross Disciplinary Research Forum project on autonomous prediction of damage scenarios in digital-twin-generated populations of civil structures. I am a student member of IEEE and ACM, and a reviewer for Engineering Research Express (IOP Publishing).

Before joining BITS I completed a B.Tech with Honours in Computer Science and Engineering at AKTU Lucknow (CGPA 8.65), interned in AI and ML at NIELIT Gorakhpur, and served as a Junior Research Fellow on the CDRF digital-twin project.

Outside the lab I write, spend time outdoors, and play volleyball.

Research Interests

Primary: Vehicle-bridge interaction modelling, computer vision, cyber-physical systems.

Application verticals: Structural health assessment, digital twins, intelligent transportation.

Methods: Transfer learning, finite element modelling, machine learning.

Vehicle-Bridge Interaction

Modelling how traffic loads and long-span bridges talk to each other for assessment.

Computer Vision

Vision in vehicles and crack/delamination-aware damage detection.

Digital Twins

Finite-element digital twins and zero-shot transfer learning for structural dynamics.

Cyber-Physical Systems

Controllers that connect sensing, simulation, and civil infrastructure.
